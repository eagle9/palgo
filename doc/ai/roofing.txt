two types of roof are found in most houses in USA.
one is concrete tiles, the other is shingle tiles.  

concrete tiles are nailed, bellow there are moisture barrier paper, with 
wooden picket to provide base to fasten the nails, 
then plywood that covers the roof wooden structure

most concrete tiles that were made 20-30 years ago are no longer manufactured
one challenge is to find the tiles from some warehouses that recovered and store the old tiles 

replacing the old shingle tiles has the similar issue to find exactly the same brand and color.
