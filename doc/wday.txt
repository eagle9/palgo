Job Description

We are looking for an outstanding Principal Software Development Engineer to join our Performance and Scalability Engineering team to manage the complex projects in Performance and Platform Engineering space. Performance Software Engineers are highly skilled engineers who debug, measure, analyze, and improve our Workday software and technology stack end to end. They are generalists with deep technical understanding of software, hardware, networking, and the Internet, who enjoy taking a holistic view of complex systems.  Application Performance is achieved by working closely with all our engineering teams throughout the release cycle. Our ultimate goal is application performance that yields an excellent end user experience and scales to meet Software as a Service Infrastructure needs.

About The Role: 

This team is responsible for the performance & scalability aspects of WDAY technology stack end to end to support HCM and Financial customers

We work with largest customers (top 5% tier of WDAY customers who use HCM and Financial applications) to ensure the go-live is successful and customer is satisfied with the performance

We closely work with technology and product organization to influence the architectural and design changes within the technology stack so that WDAY is building robust platform to support various workloads

We build pipelined automation for analyzing performance of software and hardware for certification and optimization for any changes into the platform or code
